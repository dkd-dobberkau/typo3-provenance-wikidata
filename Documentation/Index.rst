============================
Content Provenance: Wikidata
============================

:Extension key:
    provenance_wikidata

Helps editors research a fact in Wikidata from the fact dialog of the rich
text editor: find the item the fact is about, read its statements and
references, and fill the fact from a statement with one click.

This extension is part of the TYPO3 implementation of the
`Content Provenance Specification <https://github.com/dkd-dobberkau/content-provenance-spec>`__.
Installation, configuration, the editorial guide and verification for all
extensions are documented in one place, the manual of the core extension
`provenance <https://docs.typo3.org/p/cms-provenance/provenance/main/en-us/>`__.

Relevant chapters:
